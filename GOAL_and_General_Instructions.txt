GOAL:
an interactive html map, displaying the ayalonhw right of way layer, along with
the NTA right of way layer and the metropolitan traffic lights layer.
then, using a buffer around ayalonhw and NTA layers, tagging the traffic lights that are
in ayalonhw layer and those in NTA layer, but in order:
1. tagging ayalonhw first
2. tagging NTA layer later, but with removing traffic lights from ayalonhw that are also in NTA layer. this way we can assess how many traffic lights were
removed from ayalonhw and added to NTA.

Layer names:
ayalonhw right of way later: נתיבי איילון.geojson
NTA right of way later: רשויות תמרור אחרות.geojson
need to add a filter by column "רשות תמרור מקומית"="נתע"
traffic lights layer: רמזורים במטרופויט.geojson
