Traffic lights are tagged by a buffer around each right-of-way layer — Ayalon Highways first, then NTA. Lights falling in both are removed from Ayalon and reassigned to NTA. Hover for a snippet, click for full details.

Buffer distance

A light is tagged to a layer when it lies within this distance of the layer's right of way.
